This repo is public, so a few things are not in git. Put them back before you rely on the machine:

- ~/.creds
- ~/.ssh/ (private keys and config for openclaw, pi, and workbox)
- gh auth login   (~/.config/gh/hosts.yml is not in git)
- ~/.kube/config
- claude login     (~/.claude/.credentials.json is not in git)
- grok login       (~/.grok/auth.json and mcp_credentials.json are not in git)
- In ~/.creds, export TAILNET_MAGICDNS_SUFFIX and TAILSCALE_WORKBOX_IPV4
- LINEAR_API_KEY in ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.claude-tmux.daemon.plist
  (the plist is installed without it)
